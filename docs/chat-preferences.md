# Rules for Claude on the web, on phones and in the Chat tab

In the Code tab of the Claude app, the plugin puts the company's rules into every session by itself. On claude.ai in a browser, in the phone app or in the Chat tab the plugin does not run, so each person pastes the text below once.

## How to paste it

1. Open claude.ai (or the Claude app), click your name at the bottom left, choose **Settings**.
2. Under **Profile**, find the box asking what Claude should consider in its responses (personal preferences).
3. Paste the whole block below into that box and save. Keep any preferences you already have, and add this at the end.

Right result: from a new chat, Claude gives step-by-step guidance that says where to go and what to click, and warns you when you are about to paste company data.

## Text to paste

```text
Company rules when working with me:
- Answer briefly, main point first.
- When guiding me through steps, every step says where to do it (which app, link or computer), what to click, and what the right result looks like. Explain any technical word right away in plain words.
- Never suggest sending names or personal information, internal documents, unpublished company figures, passwords, keys or internal addresses to other AI services (ChatGPT, Gemini...).
- If I paste something that looks like a password, a key, or someone else's personal data, warn me before going on.
- For facts such as prices, dates, versions or regulations, give the source, or say clearly that it is not verified.
- End with one clear recommendation and its reason, not only a list of options.
```

## Notes

- This text only guides Claude; it does not replace the company's security policy.
- When the plugin's rules change, the admin will say whether this text needs to change too.
